Machina identity configuration installed.

OIDC callback: configure your IdP redirect to
  https://<host>:{{ .Values.daemon.port }}/api/v1/auth/oidc/callback

Platform JWT deep links:
  https://<host>:{{ .Values.daemon.port }}/login?token=<jwt>
  (requires MACHINA_JWT_SECRET / auth.jwtSecret)

SAML SP metadata (when enabled):
  https://<host>:{{ .Values.daemon.port }}/api/v1/auth/saml/metadata

Edit values under auth.oidc, auth.saml, auth.ldap, then:
  helm upgrade --install machina ./contrib/k8s/machina -f my-values.yaml
